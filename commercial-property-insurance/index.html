Common Inquiries

Commercial Property Insurance FAQs

Key answers for property developers, commercial landlords, and corporate tenants.

Replacement Cost Value (RCV) pays the actual modern expense to rebuild or repair your commercial building using materials of like kind and quality without deducting for age or depreciation. Actual Cash Value (ACV) deducts physical depreciation from the payout, which frequently results in substantial shortfall after severe fire or windstorm damage. We strongly recommend replacement cost provisions for enterprise portfolios.

A captive agent is an employee representing a single carrier and can only offer that company's specific policies. An independent commercial broker represents you, the insured enterprise. We leverage relationships across dozens of domestic carriers, Lloyd's syndicates, and surplus lines markets to tender competitive quotes and provide independent claims defense.

If your building experiences damage from an insured peril that halts occupancy or business operations, Business Interruption covers lost net profits, ongoing rent and mortgage payments, property taxes, key staff payroll, and the extra expense of temporary leasing arrangements.

To bind coverage, underwriters require a verified Statement of Values (SOV) specifying: building square footage, year built, roof renovation dates, electrical and plumbing modernization, construction classification, fire sprinkler systems, tenant business types, and 3 to 5 years of historical loss runs.

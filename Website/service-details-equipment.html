Underwriting Considerations & Premium Drivers

Commercial Property Insurance FAQs

Actual Cash Value (ACV) deducts depreciation from your claim payout based on the building or roof's age. Replacement Cost (RCV) pays the full current cost to rebuild the structure with materials of like kind and quality without depreciation deductions, ensuring you aren't left with an unexpected capital deficit.
An Agreed Value endorsement suspends the dreaded "coinsurance penalty" clause. With standard coinsurance, if your building is insured for less than 80% or 90% of its true market replacement cost at the time of a loss, the carrier slashes your claim payout proportionally. Shieldora secures Agreed Value endorsements so you are paid in full up to policy limits.
Standard property insurance only pays to rebuild what existed before the loss. However, local building departments frequently enforce modern environmental, seismic, ADA accessibility, and fire sprinkler codes when rebuilding. Ordinance or Law covers the huge extra expense required to bring the reconstructed building into modern municipal compliance.
While excluded by basic forms, Shieldora can either endorse Difference in Conditions (DIC) flood and earthquake sub-limits directly into your master commercial property policy or place specialized excess surplus layers through the London and domestic markets.

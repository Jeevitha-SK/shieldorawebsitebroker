Underwriting Diagnostics

The Shieldora Commercial Property Risk Assessment Matrix

Standard insurance applications only ask for square footage and fire hydrants. Our proprietary 4-pillar COPE diagnostic analyzes hidden structural vulnerabilities to present carriers with a superior risk profile—translating into premium reductions up to 25%.

Broker Insight: Commercial carriers surcharge properties by default when risk data is ambiguous. Delivering detailed COPE data eliminates underwriting safety markups.
C

Construction Classification

Fire-resistive, modified fire-resistive, or non-combustible steel framing. We verify structural load tolerances and exterior curtain-wall resilience.

O

Occupancy Risk Profile

Tenant operational hazards, combustible loading densities, commercial kitchens, hazardous chemical storage, and business operational hours.

P

Protection Systems

ESFR fire sprinkler hydraulic pressure, central station alarm telemetry, backup diesel generators, automated water shutoff valves, and security staff.

E

Exposure & Geography

Adjacent building fire separation, coastal storm surge zones, seismic fault proximity, wildfire interfaces, and municipal water supply dependability.

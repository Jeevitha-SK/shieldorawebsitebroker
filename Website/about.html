Our Heritage & Mission

Protecting Commercial Enterprises With Uncompromising Integrity.

Founded in 2008 in New York City, Shieldora Risk Solutions was built on a singular conviction: commercial property owners deserve an independent, fiduciary broker who advocates strictly for the insured, never for the carrier.

Our Mission

To empower property owners, real estate syndicates, and enterprise operators with sophisticated risk diagnostics, multi-carrier pricing leverage, and aggressive claim advocacy that preserves capital and operational continuity.

Our Vision

To redefine the commercial brokerage landscape by transforming insurance from an adversarial annual expense into a transparent, engineered asset protection strategy built on trust and technological transparency.

The Broker Advantage

Independent Guidance Backed by Global Underwriting Power

In commercial insurance, the broker's legal allegiance matters. As an independent broker, Shieldora represents you—the policyholder. We do not answer to corporate insurance carrier sales quotas.

When underwriters propose restrictive water sub-limits or inflated hurricane deductibles, our risk engineers challenge their actuarial models with empirical building data to secure favorable binding terms.

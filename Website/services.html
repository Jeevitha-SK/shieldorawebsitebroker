Comprehensive Risk Solutions

Tailored Insurance For Every Commercial Exposure

From high-rise physical structures to sudden catastrophic interruption, our independent brokerage delivers multi-layered coverage backed by A+ rated underwriters.

1. Commercial Property Insurance

Complete physical asset coverage protecting commercial buildings, roofs, tenant improvements, inventory, and fixtures from all risks including fire, wind, and civil commotion.

  • Replacement cost valuation guarantees
  • Debris removal & environmental cleanup
  • Ordinance or Law reconstruction endorsements
Learn More

2. Commercial General Liability

Shields your commercial entity from claims of third-party bodily injury, property damage, premises liability, and legal defense fees on corporate real estate grounds.

  • Up to $100M umbrella & excess towers
  • Comprehensive tenant premises slip-and-fall
  • Contractual and vendor hold-harmless coverage
Learn More

3. Business Interruption & Income

Replaces lost rental income, corporate profits, continuous mortgage payments, and essential payroll while physical property is being repaired following a covered loss.

  • Actual Loss Sustained (ALS) up to 24 months
  • Extra expense for temporary tenant relocation
  • Contingent business interruption from suppliers
Learn More

4. Equipment & Machinery

Covers mechanical, electrical, and pressure apparatus failures—including commercial HVAC chillers, backup generators, elevators, and transformers.

  • Electrical arcing and short-circuit repair
  • Emergency expediting expenses included
  • Perishable product spoilage sub-limits
Learn More

5. Fire, Flood & Natural Disaster

Dedicated catastrophic perils protection structured with customized deductible buy-backs for windstorm, hurricane, flood, storm surge, and seismic perils.

  • High-hazard flood & FEMA excess layers
  • Named windstorm deductible buy-down options
  • Parametric rapid-liquidity triggers
Learn More

6. Claims Advocacy & Management

When disaster strikes, our in-house risk adjusters champion your claim directly against carrier underwriters to accelerate recovery and minimize dispute friction.

  • 24-hour response on site
  • Independent forensic engineering reports
  • Active policyholder dispute advocacy
Learn More

7. Landlord & Rental Property Coverage

Bespoke landlord insurance addressing lease tenant defaults caused by physical damage, wrongful eviction defense, common area liability, and vacant building clauses.

  • Comprehensive tenant vandalism protection
  • Automatic 60-day vacancy permits
  • Landlord environmental & mold liability
Learn More

8. Risk Assessment & Advisory

Engineering-led building inspections, COPE hazard diagnostics, loss control consulting, and lease insurance requirement audits to optimize risk scorecards.

  • NFPA sprinkler hydraulic testing audit
  • Multi-tier lender covenant compliance review
  • Peer benchmark rate studies
Learn More

9. Warehouse & Industrial Risk Solutions

Engineered coverage for high-cube distribution centers, cold-storage facilities, cross-dock terminals, and manufacturing infrastructure facing complex operational perils.

  • NFPA 13 ESFR ceiling sprinkler compliance
  • Cross-dock & cargo staging legal liability
  • Perishable inventory spoilage sub-limits
Learn More
